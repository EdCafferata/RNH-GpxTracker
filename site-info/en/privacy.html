Who is responsible?

The IT Crowd (Ed Cafferata), based in the Netherlands, builds and runs this app.

What data?

Location data (GPS) to record your route, stored as a GPX track. No account, no name, no email address. For live weather and layers the app uses OpenWeatherMap (optional, with your own free key).

Where is it stored?

Your tracks stay on your device and, only if you choose, in your own iCloud. Nothing is shared with or sold to The IT Crowd or any third party; there are no ad trackers.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we keep no accounts or personal data on our servers, we normally hold nothing about you. Still have a question about your data or your GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection)? Email us.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy; the current version is always on this page.
