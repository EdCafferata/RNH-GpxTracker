A GPS tracker to record your sailing route on iPhone and Apple Watch — built for the Ronde om Noord-Holland. No account, no subscription, no fuss: just sail and record.

Download on the App Store

What does the app do?

  • Record your full route as a GPX track — pause, resume and merge sessions
  • OpenWeatherMap map layers (precipitation, wind, clouds) with legend over your chart
  • Speed-based map zoom + course-up; multiple map servers (incl. OpenSeaMap)
  • Add, edit, move and export waypoints
  • Apple Watch: record independently from your wrist, send the track to iPhone
  • Offline map cache — browse your sailing area in advance, even without internet
  • Export/import GPX via email, AirDrop or any other app
